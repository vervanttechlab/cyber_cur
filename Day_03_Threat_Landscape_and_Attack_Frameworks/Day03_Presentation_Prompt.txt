  2. Describe the main categories of threat actors and their motives.
  3. Explain the seven phases of the Cyber Kill Chain.
  4. Navigate MITRE ATT&CK tactics and map observed behavior to a tactic and technique.
  5. Recognize indicators of compromise (IOCs) and indicators of attack (IOAs).
