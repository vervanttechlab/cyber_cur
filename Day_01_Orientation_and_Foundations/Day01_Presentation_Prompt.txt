LEARNING OBJECTIVES (show these on Slide 2)
  1. Describe how the program is delivered: synchronous morning sessions via Google Meet and asynchronous afternoon activity work.
  2. Explain the structure of the Cyber Threat Monitoring Level I qualification and its two core units.
  3. Describe the job roles and daily tasks of a Level I cybersecurity analyst in a Security Operations Center (SOC).
  4. Explain the CIA triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) using workplace examples.
  5. Differentiate threat, vulnerability, risk, asset and incident.
  6. Identify the legal and ethical obligations of a cyber threat monitor under RA 10175 and RA 10173.
  7. Apply occupational safety and health (OSH) practices for computer-based work.

HARD RULES
1. The deck must have EXACTLY 15 slides — no more, no less. Follow the
   slide-by-slide outline below in the same order.
2. Maximum 5 bullet points per slide and maximum 12 words per bullet.
3. Every slide must include:
   a) a clear slide title,
   b) the on-slide content (bullets, table, or diagram),
   c) a suggested visual (icon, diagram, screenshot, or chart), and
   d) SPEAKER NOTES of 80–150 words written in a conversational trainer
      voice, including one check-for-understanding question where useful.
4. Use real-world, Philippine-relevant examples where possible
   (banks, BPOs, LGUs, schools, hospitals, e-wallets).
5. Stay accurate: use correct terminology from NIST, MITRE ATT&CK and the
   TESDA Competency Standards. Do not invent statistics; if a figure is
   needed, mark it as "[verify latest figure]".
6. Never show working malicious code, real exploit steps, or real
   credentials. Demonstrations stay inside an isolated training lab.
